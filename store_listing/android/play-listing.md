# ICCASA Field: Google Play listing

## Store copy

**App name:** ICCASA Field

**Short description:** Collect inclusive climate data offline and sync securely with ICCASA.

**Full description:**

ICCASA Field is the mobile collection workspace for the ICCASA monitoring and evaluation platform. It helps authorized field staff gather reliable, inclusive evidence wherever their work takes them.

- Open forms assigned to your account and review their collection requirements.
- Complete guided surveys with country, district or county, and disability information where relevant.
- Capture location, photos, and signatures when a form requests them.
- Save drafts and continue collection without a network connection.
- Review submitted records and see their review status.
- Sync queued work securely when connectivity returns.

ICCASA Field is an organization-managed app. Access is provided by ICCASA or a participating organization. A platform account and assigned forms are needed for live collection.

## Release notes

Updated field collection experience with guided forms, offline drafts, review status, and secure sync. Includes ICCASA branding and push notification support for assigned work when configured by the organization.
